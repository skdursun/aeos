# Short Incident Title
One to three sentences summarizing the event, impact, and current state.

## Context
What was happening when the incident occurred.

## Impact
- User, workflow, data, or system effect.
- Duration, scope, or severity if known.

## Response
Actions taken to contain, diagnose, or recover.

## Resolution
Current outcome and remaining follow-up.

## Use When
Use when similar operational failures, symptoms, or response decisions appear.

## Reuse Guidance
Reuse the response pattern only after confirming the same impact and cause.
